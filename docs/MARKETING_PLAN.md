# 🚀 Go-to-Market & Client Acquisition Plan

Your Salon Management Bot is complete, stable, and ready for real users. Since the product is live on Bale, the focus is now 100% on **marketing, onboarding, and generating revenue**.

Here is a simple, actionable, and low-cost marketing plan tailored for the Iranian beauty and service market.

---

## Phase 1: The Initial Spark (0 to 50 Salons)
*Goal: Get the first real users to test the system and provide testimonials.*

### 1. Direct Outreach (Cold Messaging)
- **Where:** Bale groups, Telegram channels, and Instagram pages of local salons, nail technicians, and barbers.
- **Action:** Send a friendly, short message.
- **Pitch Example:**
  > "سلام وقتتون بخیر! 🌹 برای مدیریت نوبت‌های سالنتون هنوز مشتری‌ها باید تماس بگیرن؟ ربات ما توی بله به صورت کاملاً خودکار نوبت‌دهی می‌کنه، قابلیت تنظیم بیعانه داره و روز قبل به مشتری یادآوری می‌کنه. ۱۴ روز تست کاملاً رایگان داره. دوست دارید لینکشو بفرستم امتحان کنید؟"

### 2. Leverage Your Immediate Network
- Offer the bot to your own barber or hair stylist.
- Help them set it up in person. An in-person setup ensures they use it, and their feedback will be invaluable.

---

## Phase 2: Building Trust & Presence
*Goal: Make the bot look professional so when salons search for it, they trust it.*

### 3. Create an Official Channel
- Set up a channel on Bale/Telegram (e.g., `@SalonBot_Official`).
- Use the AI-generated images from `docs/PROMPTS.md` to design a professional logo and banners.
- Post short, 30-second screen recordings showing:
  - How easy it is for a manager to set their working hours.
  - How fast a customer can book a slot at 2:00 AM.

### 4. Highlight the "Aha!" Moment
- Salon owners are busy. Focus your marketing on **saving time** and **reducing no-shows**.
- Emphasize features like: 
  - "نوبت‌دهی ۲۴ ساعته بدون نیاز به پاسخگویی شما" (24/7 booking without your response).
  - "ارسال خودکار پیامک/پیام یادآوری برای کاهش فراموشی نوبت" (Automated reminders).

---

## Phase 3: Scaling & Viral Growth (50+ Salons)
*Goal: Make your users bring you more users.*

### 5. Referral Program (Affiliate)
- Beauty professionals know other beauty professionals.
- Run a campaign: "معرفی کنید، اشتراک رایگان بگیرید" (Refer and get free subscription).
- If a salon invites a colleague who purchases a subscription, add 1 or 2 months of free subscription to the inviter's account.

### 6. Partner with Beauty Educators
- Find instructors teaching hairdressing, nail tech, or makeup on Instagram/Bale.
- Give the instructors a **Free 1-Year Premium Account**.
- In exchange, ask them to introduce your bot to their students as the best tool for starting their new business. Beginners are the most eager to adopt cheap, automated software.

---

## 💡 Daily Routine for the Next 30 Days
To gain traction, consistency is key:
1. **Spend 30 minutes** sending DMs to 15-20 new salons every day.
2. **Spend 15 minutes** following up with salons that started the 14-day trial to see if they need help setting up their services.
3. **Post 1 feature showcase** per week in your official channel.
